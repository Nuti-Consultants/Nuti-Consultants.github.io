Privacy Policy

Nuti Consultants · Effective Date: 11 May 2026

Scope. This Privacy Policy applies to all applications published by Nuti Consultants (developer account "nuticonsultants") on Google Play, including but not limited to PacketScope, and to any future application released under the same developer account unless that application provides its own superseding policy.

Nuti Consultants ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. We design our applications to function without collecting personal information from our users, and this policy explains in clear terms what that means in practice. This document is written to comply with the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as well as Google Play's User Data policy.

1. Information Collection

Our applications are designed to operate without collecting personal data. Specifically, Nuti Consultants applications do not collect, transmit, or process any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, postal address).
  • Account credentials (we do not maintain user accounts or require sign-in).
  • Device identifiers (advertising ID, IMEI, MAC address, Android ID, hardware serial).
  • Location data (precise or approximate).
  • Contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, photos, or microphone input.
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, crash reports sent to us, or behavioural metrics.
  • Cookies or similar tracking technologies.
  • Advertising identifiers or any data used for ad targeting.

The only inputs our applications process are files or content that you explicitly open from your device's storage, and only while you are actively viewing them in the app. For example, PacketScope reads packet-capture files (.pcap / .pcapng) that you select via the Android system file picker; nothing in those files is sent anywhere outside your device.

2. Data Usage

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not use it for any purpose. Any data your device processes within one of our applications is used solely for the on-screen functionality the application provides at that moment (for example, dissecting a packet capture you opened so the app can display its contents to you).

We do not:

  • Profile users or build behavioural models.
  • Sell, rent, or share data with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.
  • Use any data for advertising. Our applications currently contain no advertisements, and we do not integrate any advertising SDKs. Should this change in a future application or version, this policy will be updated and the affected application will request your consent where required by applicable law.

3. Data Storage

All processing performed by our applications takes place locally on your device. Files you open are accessed read-only. Where an application produces an output (for example, PacketScope's "Export filtered packets as pcap" feature), the resulting file is written to the application's private cache or to a location you choose, and only leaves your device if you explicitly share it using Android's share sheet to a destination of your choosing.

We do not operate any servers that store user data, and we do not receive copies of any data from your device.

4. Permissions

Each of our applications requests only the minimum Android permissions needed to perform its on-device function. Common examples include:

  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (capped at Android 12 / API 32): required on older Android versions so the app can open files you select from device storage. Android 13+ uses the Storage Access Framework, which does not require this permission.
  • Storage Access Framework (no runtime permission required): how files are opened on modern Android versions.

Our applications do not request the INTERNET permission unless a specific feature requires it, in which case its purpose will be disclosed in the application's Play Store listing. PacketScope, in particular, does not request the INTERNET permission and operates entirely offline.

5. Third-Party Services

Our applications do not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. We use only open-source libraries that execute in-process on your device and do not transmit any data externally. Where libraries are bundled (for example, the gopacket Go library and Jetpack Compose UI toolkit inside PacketScope), they operate purely on-device with no network calls initiated by us.

Should any future application require a third-party service that processes personal data, that application will be accompanied by a supplementary privacy notice naming the service, the data shared, the purpose, and your rights with respect to that processing.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Because Nuti Consultants does not collect or process personal data about you, most rights under the GDPR (right of access, rectification, erasure, data portability, restriction, objection) and the CCPA (right to know, delete, opt out of sale, non-discrimination) have no data to act on. There is no profile to access, no record to delete, no sale of personal information to opt out of.

You may nevertheless contact us at any time (see Section 9) to confirm in writing that no data about you is held, or to ask any privacy-related question regarding our applications.

7. Children's Privacy

Our applications are general-purpose developer and productivity tools. They are not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable minimum). We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in error (which would in any case not be retained), please contact us so we can confirm no such data exists.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our applications, legal requirements, or industry practice. Updates will be posted at the same URL with the "Effective Date" at the top of this document revised accordingly. Material changes — for example, the introduction of any data collection by any Nuti Consultants application — will be communicated through the application's release notes and, where required by law, through an in-app notice prompting your consent.
